## Full description (4,000 characters max)

```
IA cave à vin keeps track of your wine cellar, bottle by bottle, and tells you what to open and when.

SNAP THE LABEL
Take a photo of the bottle: producer, cuvée, appellation, vintage, colour, alcohol and volume are read from the label. The identification service then completes the record with grape varieties, drinking window, food pairings and the producer's notes. Every value found cites its source: nothing is made up, and information that cannot be found stays empty. You approve or correct each field, and anything you entered by hand is never overwritten.
The barcode and the vintage can also be read live with the camera, without a network.

MANAGE YOUR STOCK
- Lots by bottle size, owner, location and purchase price.
- Purchases, openings, transfers and inventory corrections, all dated and explained.
- Locations as a tree: cellar, cabinet, rack with a grid view, shelf.
- Several owners in the same cellar.

KNOW WHAT TO DRINK
- "To drink" screen: wines in their drinking window, the ones to drink soon first.
- "Past" tab: bottles past their drinking window, kept apart.
- "Drinking priority" sort and years to peak shown on every wine.
- Ageing rules you can edit by colour, region and classification.
- Monthly alert: wines reaching their peak or the end of their window.
- Reverse pairing: pick a dish, the app suggests wines from your cellar.

TASTE AND SHARE
- Tasting history with score, dish, comment and photos.
- Wine record as a PDF or an image, named after the wine.
- Shopping list to buy a wine again or note a wine spotted elsewhere.

ANALYSE
- Statistics: bottles by colour, region, vintage, ageing status and drinking priority, purchase value of the cellar.
- Price history: market price found online or a dated price you enter yourself.
- PDF catalogue of the whole cellar.
- Excel export and import, with a dry run before import.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
The cellar lives on your phone and works offline, without an account: list, records, stock, statistics, PDF and Excel. No ads, no audience tracking. Only label photos go to the identification service, after your consent, and they are never stored there. Full backup and restore in one file.

GOOD TO KNOW
Photo identification and price checks use an online service run by Apexus, with a free account: your email address and a code received by email, no password. 10 identifications and 10 price checks a month. Without an account, all cellar management works and records stay "to enrich" until you sign in.
The app is in English and French. Texts written by the identification service (producer notes, pairings) are in French for now.

For adults only. The app does not sell alcohol.
Alcohol abuse is dangerous for your health. Drink responsibly.
```
